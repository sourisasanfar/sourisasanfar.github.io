Research

Human decisions in pharmaceutical supply chains

Northeastern University, NSF Award 2228510, advised by Prof. Jacqueline Griffin

Drug shortages trigger defensive ordering that can make shortages worse. I combine human‑in‑the‑loop experiments with agent‑based simulation to understand why. In a 178‑participant between‑subjects experiment, I studied how upstream information sharing changes wholesaler ordering, then used PCA, clustering and Hidden Markov Models to identify ordering archetypes and built simulated agents validated against the behavioral data.

Clinical NLP for patient safety

FARIL Lab, Mass General Brigham / Harvard Medical School

Falls are badly captured by billing codes. I design hybrid pipelines that pair machine‑learning classifiers (AdaBoost, XGBoost, ClinicalBERT) with context‑aware rules for negation, timing, and risk versus actual events. Across 26,975 emergency‑department notes from 670 patients with type 2 diabetes, this post‑processing cut false positives substantially while keeping sensitivity.
